# Scientific contract and abstention

Every finding is typed: `SYNTHETIC_CALIBRATION`, `PERFORMANCE_CROSSOVER`, `PERFORMANCE_CLIFF`, `CORRECTNESS_BOUNDARY`, `MEMORY_OR_RESOURCE_BOUNDARY`, or `UNSTABLE_OR_INCONCLUSIVE`. Exact detector subtype and operational criteria are recorded. `SCALING_REGIME_CHANGE` is not automatically confirmed by curve fitting: finite-size fit results are separate exploratory evidence. `NO_CONFIRMED_BOUNDARY` is a valid negative conclusion, including a genuinely constant work-count control.

A completed valid candidate must first agree with the independent output oracle. Native plugin crashes, incorrect results, validation errors, timeouts and resource exhaustion remain visible and cannot masquerade as fast timings. Confirmation always uses fresh case IDs and a frozen structural context. A single-run statistically supported performance finding may be `CONFIRMED`, but it is **still observational, CPU/environment-specific and not causal**. Witness reduction must preserve the chosen predicate and recheck independently; failed minimization has a distinct status from successful minimization.

A synthetic deterministic work transition is an intentionally **engineered detector calibration**, not a discovered law of algorithms, novel mechanism or SOTA result. A fitted finite n growth curve is never a proof of asymptotic Big-O. Lack of a confirmed boundary does not mean absence of one outside the searched domain. Experimental conditions and multiplicity limitations must accompany any proposed real-world finding.
